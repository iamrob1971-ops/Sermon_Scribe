Study Assistant & Guide v2.7 Multimodal

Working Modes & SharePoint Sync

Sermon Mode: Built for expository preaching and sermon notes. Saves into the Devotional_Topics list. Entering a Topic ID and clicking 📥 Pull loads existing records. Saving appends/replaces into KeyHighlights_HTML (Summary) and StudyNotes_HTML (Transcript + Images), while chat discussions append directly to Other_SummariesForStudy.

Devotional Mode: Built for Spurgeon's Faith's Checkbook. Entering a Target Row ID and clicking 📥 Pull loads Spurgeon's text into the transcript area. Saving posts to Resources (AdditionalCommentary), Companion_Voices, and Family_Insights, while chat discussions append directly to AINotes.

Image Snips, Multi-Images & On-Demand OCR

Chat Snips: Paste multiple images (`Ctrl + V`) into the chat box or click 🖼️. Gemini analyzes multiple book pages, charts, or Greek/Hebrew text simultaneously alongside your question.

Raw Input Snips & OCR: Paste images into the main editor or click 🖼️ Attach Snip. You can keep images as visual diagrams, OR click `📝 Extract Text` directly on any chip to convert physical book pages into editable text!

Recording & Audio Slicing

Live Mic: Real-time browser speech recognition with room-echo deduplication and a clean pause guard.

📁 Attach Audio: Slices any MP3, M4A, or WAV into 5-minute chunks in browser memory and transcribes sequentially via Gemini.
